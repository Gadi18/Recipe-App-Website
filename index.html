Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have a free trial?

Yes! Start with our Free plan and upgrade to Pro whenever you need cost tracking and unlimited recipes. Pro users get a 7-day free trial.

Can I import recipes from other apps?

Yes. You can import recipes from websites, paste recipe text directly, or use our AI scanner to photograph recipes from books and cards.

How does the cost tracking work?

Set prices for your ingredients (manually or by uploading invoices). RecipePro calculates the exact cost per serving and per recipe automatically.

Can I share recipes with others?

You can share individual recipes with friends. Family sharing and team features are coming soon.

What if I need help?

Email us at support@recipepro.app or visit our help center. We typically respond within 24 hours.

Is my data secure?

Your recipes and data are encrypted and stored securely. We never sell your data, and you can export your recipes anytime.

Privacy Policy

Data Collection

RecipePro collects the following data to provide and improve our service: recipes and ingredients you enter, account information, usage analytics, and device information. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Storage

Your recipes and personal data are stored securely on encrypted servers. You maintain ownership of all your data and can export or delete it at any time. We comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations.

Third-Party Services

RecipePro uses third-party services including cloud storage providers and analytics tools. These services are contractually bound to protect your privacy and maintain confidentiality.

AI and Machine Learning

Our AI recipe scanner uses OpenAI's vision capabilities to extract recipe information from photos. We do not train models on your recipes—extracted data is processed only to recognize ingredients and quantities.

Cookies and Tracking

RecipePro uses minimal cookies to enhance your experience and does not use invasive tracking. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting core functionality.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification. Your continued use of RecipePro constitutes acceptance of any changes.

Contact Us

For privacy concerns or data requests, contact us at privacy@recipepro.app. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 30 days.
